A classroom without borders

Teach anywhere. Learn from everywhere.

Prod99 is a global classroom where teachers share their craft and students join from anywhere in the world. Set goals, track every study session, and trade ideas in a community that keeps you moving.

See how it works

Everything a borderless classroom needs.

Session timer

Pick a subject, start the clock, and Prod99 logs the block automatically once you stop. No manual entry, no guessing how long you actually studied.

Assignment tracker

Every deadline lives in one list, sorted by what's due first. Mark things done as you go and watch the list get shorter instead of longer.

Weekly streaks

A simple view of which days you actually showed up, so you can see a pattern instead of relying on a vague sense of how the week went.

Goal tracker

Set a goal, nudge your progress, and watch the bar fill. Teachers can share milestones; students make them their own.

Community posts

Share a tip, ask a question, or post a lesson update. Teachers and students trade ideas in one friendly feed.

Live classes, any timezone

Teachers host sessions and students join from far away, with recordings and notes waiting when the clock does not line up.

Progress insights

Weekly study charts and streaks turn effort into a pattern you can see and improve.
